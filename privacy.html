Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Hill Hub is a personal home-automation project for one household. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody outside the household can sign in to it.

What it accesses

Hill Hub accesses Google account data belonging only to the project owner: their own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Docs. It also reads the household's own calendars. It never accesses anyone else's Google account.

Where it is stored

Data is processed on a private Raspberry Pi on the owner's home network, and stored there and in the owner's own Google account.

Services it uses

To run the automations, parts of the data are processed by services the owner uses directly:

  • Google (Gmail, Calendar, Sheets, Drive and Docs), where the data is held
  • Anthropic's Claude API, which reads and summarises it
  • The owner's private Discord server, for messages to the household
  • The owner's Notion workspace, for recipes

Sharing

There is no third-party sharing beyond those services. The data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or analytics.
